# MUIC Case Club: Communication Templates

>

>
> **[Interest]** Founded in 2018, MUIC Case Club is Mahidol University International College's largest consultancy-based student organization, with [40+] members from [business, science, engineering, and media] majors. We organize the HSBC Thailand Business Case Competition in partnership with HSBC and host industry sessions such as [“Pathway to Consulting Insight from BCG,” which drew 105 students].
>
> **[Desire]** As a partner for [Event], [Company] would receive:
> - [Brand visibility across [N] participating universities and our social channels]
> - [Direct access to top student talent through a networking/recruitment session]
> - [A seat on the judging panel for [Round]]

> **[Action]** We would welcome a 20-minute call to discuss how this partnership could support [Company]'s goals. Could you let us know a convenient time before **[Month D, YYYY]**?
>
> Sincerely,
>
> [Full Name]
> [Role], MUIC Case Club
> Mahidol University International College
> muic.caseclub@gmail.com | [Phone]

## 4. Not-Selected Notice, e.g., Internal Round (Register D · Bad-News Structure)

> Dear [First name],
>
> **[Buffer]** Thank you for the effort you and your team, [Team Name] Consulting, put into the HSBC [Year] Internal Round. The judges were impressed by [a specific strength, e.g., your customer segmentation analysis].
>
> **[Reasons]** This year we received [N] strong submissions for [N] representative slots. Judges evaluated each team on situational analysis, strategy, financial impact, and delivery.
>
> **[News]** After careful deliberation, [Team Name] has not been selected to represent MUIC in this year's competition.
>
> **[Alternative]** We would love for you to stay involved. You are warmly invited to join the **HSBC [Year] Volunteerism Program** and our weekly up-skilling sessions every [Tuesday], where the judges' detailed feedback on your deck will also be shared.
>
> **[Positive close]** Many of our past winners were not selected on their first attempt. We look forward to seeing you compete again.
>
> Best regards,
>
> [Full Name]
> [Role], MUIC Case Club
